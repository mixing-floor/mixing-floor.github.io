§ 01

Abstract

Automatic data-mixing methods are usually evaluated with pretraining sweeps from 70M to billions of parameters, which leaves a basic question open: how much compute does it take to tell these methods apart?

We study this on a pool split by provenance, 9 real Pile subsets and 9 synthetic instruction corpora, where every method receives the same per-domain validation-loss signal and we record how it shifts the synthetic share of the mixture. At 160M parameters (2.30 × 1017 FLOPs per run), the adaptive methods separate into two families across three seeds: loss-monotone reweighters move mass toward the harder real text (Group-DRO to a synthetic share of 0.20, DoReMi-style to 0.37), while interaction and gradient estimators stay near the 0.50 start. The split follows a training-induced loss gap that makes real text the high-loss provenance.

Two cheaper tiers locate a compute floor: the split is already clear at 1.2 × 1016 FLOPs, but at 2.5 × 1014 FLOPs it becomes seed-dominated, with Group-DRO showing no net direction and DoReMi-style reversing. Held-out test loss gives the least-moving method, Aioli, the lowest mean, a result we hedge given n = 3 and the confound of online training. The protocol diagnoses which mixture a policy selects, not final model quality; all code and artifacts are released at github.com/mixing-floor/compute-floor.

  1. Two families, read off one number.

    Given the identical loss signal, loss-monotone reweighters cut the synthetic share; Aioli, DoGE and Skill-It hold it near 0.50. The split is tight across all three seeds.

  2. The trajectory decides, not the optimizer.

    At round 0 the provenances are tied (2.94 vs 2.90 nats). Online training opens a gap of about one nat, real 4.09 vs synthetic 3.06, and a score that rises with own loss must chase it.

  3. A floor, three orders below a frontier run.

    The split already holds at 1.2 × 1016 FLOPs on pythia-70m and is seed-dominated at 2.5 × 1014. Below the floor, report a run as inconclusive, not as a null.

§ 02

Protocol: freeze the signal, vary the rule

Every method runs the identical protocol, so the only variable across runs is the method itself. Mixing is sampling: each step draws a batch by multinomial(batch, p) over 18 domains, and every online method moves p off one signal, per-domain validation loss. We log p each round and read off the mass on the synthetic domains.

§ 03

Turn the compute dial

The same six methods, the same pool, at three budgets. At the Full and Intermediate tiers the family split is clean. At the Minimal tier it falls apart: Group-DRO points nowhere on net and DoReMi-style climbs to 0.75.

§ 04

Where the floor sits

The floor is the lowest budget at which the loss-monotone methods agree in direction across seeds. Group-DRO's three Intermediate-tier seeds land in [0.234, 0.239]; at the Minimal tier they scatter to 0.996, 0.253 and 0.262. Both cheaper tiers run pythia-70m, so the floor is a budget effect at fixed model size, not a size effect.

Vetting a new reweighter: run it beside Stratified and Group-DRO on a provenance-split pool at the Intermediate budget or above, over three seeds, about 1.1 × 1017 FLOPs in all. A share that falls with Group-DRO's, as tightly, marks a loss-monotone policy.
§ 05

Loss is not value

What separates the families is how much the update's score leans on a domain's own loss. All six methods share one exponentiated-gradient update on the simplex and differ only in the score they feed it:

Shared update

A loss-monotone score is forced toward the highest-loss provenance; an interaction or gradient score is not, because its move depends on cross-domain transfer, not on ℓ. Which provenance is higher-loss is an empirical input, and it fixes the direction of mass flow:

A-priori prediction

Decompose a domain's loss as , irreducible entropy plus a reducible gap. A raw-loss method cannot separate the two terms, so it chases whichever provenance carries the higher loss whether or not that loss is reducible. DoReMi's excess-loss centering is the standard fix, which is why it moves less than raw Group-DRO. DoGE's inner product carries a self term , so its near-uniform behaviour holds because cross terms dominate on this pool, not by construction.

§ 06

Where the mass goes

It is not provenance the loss-movers chase but loss. Group-DRO piles onto high-loss real domains (Pile-CC 0.164, OpenWebText2 0.150) and starves easy synthetic ones (Code-Alpaca 0.004), yet also trims low-loss real domains such as Github and ArXiv. Aioli stays in a tight band around uniform.

§ 07

Held-out test loss, hedged

Aioli has the lowest mean, 3.60 nats, and beats DoGE and Skill-It on every seed. Against Stratified, DoReMi-style and Group-DRO it lands within seed scatter, and no aggressive loss-mover pulls ahead of the uniform control by more than that scatter.

With n = 3 this describes average behaviour, not an ordering: models are scored after online training rather than a clean static retrain, so mixture choice is confounded with online dynamics and with Aioli's probe-driven trajectory. The runner's --static-eval path removes that confound.

§ 09

Reproduce it

One self-contained runner regenerates every reported number. Data streams from the Hugging Face Hub; models continue from pretrained Pythia checkpoints.

Setup
git clone https://github.com/mixing-floor/compute-floor.git
cd compute-floor
pip install -r requirements.txt
Smoke test · Minimal tier (--config SUPER_LIGHT), minutes
python Code/run_experiment.py --config SUPER_LIGHT --seeds 0 --scenario S2_9R_9S --eval-test --out-dir runs/main
Reported run · Full tier (--config HEAVY), 3 seeds
python Code/run_experiment.py --config HEAVY --seeds 0 1 2 --scenario S2_9R_9S --eval-test --out-dir runs/main
python Code/visualize_results.py --out-dir runs/main --fig-dir runs/figures

Compare your runs/main against the released Results/main. Signal-level diagnostics that log DoGE's gradient inner products, Skill-It's influence graph and Aioli's interaction blocks are in Results/diagnostics.

§ 10

What this does not show

Behaviour, not quality

The protocol records which mixture each policy picks. No downstream task metric is reported, and no method is claimed to train a better model.

Online, not static

Test loss is measured after online training, which tangles mixture choice with training dynamics. A static retrain on each final mixture would settle it.

One scenario, small n

Scenario S2 at three seeds. The split and L2 ordering are tight apart from DoGE, which wanders from 0.453 to 0.557 between seeds.

Provenance ⇄ template

The synthetic class is defined by instruction format, so provenance and template are confounded. Whether the split survives past 160M, or vanishes on an all-real pool as predicted, is open.
